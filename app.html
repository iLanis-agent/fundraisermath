Fundraiser Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a fundraiser with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the items to sell for a goal, the share each seller takes, the boxes, and the profit. It uses no named constants. The goal, the price, the cost, the items, the sellers, the items a box and the fixed costs are yours to decide and read off your own plan and receipts. It applies the counting as written, so it has not met your buyers and does not know what will sell, any fee, tax or permit rule, and it gives no verdict that the goal can be reached or that the plan is wise. Every number here is your own entry.

The goal truth

Goal: the price less the cost gives the profit on each item, and the goal divided by it, rounded up, gives the items to sell.

The seller truth

Sellers: the items divided among the sellers, with the items left over.

The box truth

Boxes: the items divided by the items a box, rounded up, with the spaces spare.

The profit truth

Profit: the items sold times the price less the cost each, less the fixed costs.

Fundraiser Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
